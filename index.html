Website performance & Google Ads forecast

Turning tour interest into measurable bookings.

This presentation combines current Wix website behaviour, sales data and adjustable Google Search Ads assumptions to show how paid traffic could generate tour bookings, checkout revenue and potential wine add-on sales.

Budget range: £300–£500/month 365-day AOV: £68.17 per transaction 605 email contacts Seasonal peak: spring, summer, autumn

Important modelling note

The average order value is treated as revenue per transaction, not spend per visitor or spend per person.

For tour bookings, one transaction may represent one ticket or a group booking. The average group size is therefore used to estimate people through the door and possible wine add-ons — not to multiply the tour booking revenue.

Core assumption: guided tour and self-guided values are per booking transaction.

3. Google Search Ads click-through rates

CTR explains how impressions turn into paid visits.

Ad spend and CPC estimate the number of clicks. CTR then shows roughly how many search impressions are needed to generate those clicks.

Search Ad CTRInterpretation
2%Poor / weak relevance
3–5%Average
5–8%Good
8–12%Very good
12%+Exceptional, often brand-led or very high intent
Example
£400 ÷ £1.20 = 333 clicks
At 5% CTR, 333 clicks need roughly 6,660 impressions.
At 8% CTR, 333 clicks need roughly 4,163 impressions.
At 10% CTR, 333 clicks need roughly 3,330 impressions.

Recommended starting point

Start controlled, then scale what proves itself.

A sensible first test would use a £300–£500 monthly budget focused on high-intent search terms around vineyard tours, English wine tasting, wine tasting gift vouchers and local tourism searches. The main goal should be completed tour bookings, with secondary tracking for wine purchases, gift vouchers and email sign-ups.

Best first objective: prove a reliable cost per completed booking. Once the campaign has enough conversion data, increase budget into the search terms and ads that produce bookings at the strongest return.
